# Demo script (about 80 seconds)

Record at 1440×900 in a fresh browser window, with the browser zoom at 100%. Before you start, open the **Pipeline** page. If the board is empty, click **Add example briefs** first, so five or six briefs sit in the New column.

The brief is a curd brand from Bangalore. It's a good demo because one brief shows strong matches, reasons, a real gap and how the gap gets closed.

---

**0:00–0:10 · The problem**
*On screen:* the Pipeline board. Point at the "Sample data" label at the top right.
> "First Batch matches brands with labs, technologists and factories by hand, and that takes weeks. This is a working tool for that: every brief on one board, from first match to done. The partner data here is made up."

**0:10–0:22 · A new brief**
*On screen:* click **New brief**. Choose **Fill with an example… → High-protein curd cups**. Change the name to `Curd cups for Daily Dahi` and replace the stuck box with:
`Our curd turns sour after a week, and no factory will do a small first run.`
Click **Next**.
> "A brand fills in a short brief: chilled dairy, 300 kg first batch, Bangalore, and where they're stuck in their own words."

**0:22–0:30 · What we understood**
*On screen:* the three tags: shelf life, kitchen to factory, food safety. Click **Find partners**.
> "AI turns that note into tags from a fixed list. It never picks partners. The team can fix the tags first."

**0:30–0:42 · The path and the reasons**
*On screen:* the brief lands on the board with its panel open. Run the cursor down the five steps, then open **Testing lab** and click **Why 82?** on Mysore Dairy Research Lab.
> "The panel shows every partner this project needs, in order. Each score breaks down into reasons that add up to the total, so anyone can check the ranking."

**0:42–0:52 · Logging an outcome**
*On screen:* click **Pick** on Mysore Dairy Research Lab, choose **Project went ahead**, type `Shelf-life study done in 3 weeks`, and click **Log outcome**. Then open **Food technologist** and click **Pick** on Vikram Shetty without logging anything, so the intro shows as sent. Close the panel.
> "When an intro goes out or works out, the team logs it. The ranking learns from it, and the card moves along the board on its own."

**0:52–1:05 · The gap**
*On screen:* the card now sits in **Intros out**. Open it again and open **Contract manufacturer**. Point at the amber box, then click **Recruit**.
> "And it's honest about what's missing. No manufacturer in the network does chilled dairy at 300 kg."

**1:05–1:20 · Closing the loop**
*On screen:* the Recruiting page, with the row highlighted. Click **Add partner** on it. The form is already filled in from the gap. Type the name `Hebbal Dairy Co-packers` and click **Add to network and re-match**. Point at the green box: two curd briefs can now pick a contract manufacturer.
> "Gaps from every brief roll up into one recruiting list. Sign the partner, add them here, and every brief that was waiting gets matched again. That's the loop."

---

**Before recording:** briefs, outcomes and added partners are saved in the database, so test clicks stay. Clear the `briefs`, `outcomes` and `added_partners` tables in Neon first for a clean board.
